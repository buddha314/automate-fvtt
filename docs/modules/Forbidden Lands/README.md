# Forbidden Lands

We will provide a one-off advanture for Forbidden Lands.

Before the raising of the Blood Mist, the PCs Village (a Keep) has been cultivating a fertile garden a few hours walk away. They were able to collect a for two hours and bring bounty back. Now, the garden has stopped producing. The players are asked to investigate. They have three days or starvation will set in for their friends and families.

Why? Members of desperate village have been plundering at night. Noone is sure if the mist is gone, but these villagers have decided to risk staying in teh grove at night, travelling there and back during the day.
