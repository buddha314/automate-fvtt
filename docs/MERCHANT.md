# MERCHANTS

Merchants can be of various types. Smiths produce armor and weapons. Alchemists produce consumable magic items. Tinkerers can produce bespoke technologies. Traders can introduce exotic materials and equipment, alleviating the need for risky quests.

Merchant will be attracted to various Keeps by evaluating the basic Keep Metrics

## Mechant Mechanics

The Merchant shop has a flexible stock system. It may be set by the GM or randomly generated by the economic system. Merchants have a restock interval that may be a function of the Keep they inhabit.  For instance, larger Keeps may refresh their merchants more often. The refresh rate has a default set by the Keep

### GM / Module Configuration

The GM can assign a list of items this merchant may carry. The GM may set a randomization routine, for instance Mulitnomial inversely proportional to item value. As a Merchant's capacity increases, they will increase their probability of more rare items.  Of course, this can be overridden by GM or Module fiat.
